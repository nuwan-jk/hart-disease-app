Medical Document 14
Topic: Arrhythmia

Symptoms and Presentation:
Patients with Arrhythmia typically present with the following symptoms: Fluttering in the chest, racing heartbeat (tachycardia), slow heartbeat (bradycardia), dizziness, fainting.
Diagnosis often involves ECG, echocardiogram, and blood tests. Early detection is critical for improving patient outcomes.
Treatment options may include lifestyle changes, medications (such as beta-blockers or ACE inhibitors), and in severe cases, surgical interventions.
